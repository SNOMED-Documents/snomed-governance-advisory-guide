# About the Member Forum

The Member Forum (MF) is an important group where representatives from SNOMED International Member countries collaborate, share experiences, and provide advice to the organization.

## What is the Member Forum? <a href="#h.guc8qblizrwn_l" id="h.guc8qblizrwn_l"></a>

The MF is a collaborative group made up of representatives from SNOMED International Member countries.

Its primary purpose is to enable Members to share experiences, collaborate on implementation, and provide advice to SNOMED International on matters relating to the adoption and use of SNOMED CT.

According to the Terms of Reference, the Member Forum acts as a non-voting advisory body to the CEO and Senior Management Team (SMT) and serves as a global collaboration hub supporting SNOMED CT adoption and implementation worldwide.

The Member Forum helps connect national SNOMED CT programs and supports the global exchange of implementation knowledge and practical experience.

Through this collaboration, the Member Forum contributes to:

* Advancing global adoption of SNOMED CT
* Sharing implementation experiences and best practices
* Identifying common challenges and opportunities
* Providing implementation insights and recommendations to SNOMED International
* Engaging with the wider standards and digital health community

Representatives are typically leaders involved in their country’s SNOMED CT implementation program, National Release Centre, or digital health strategy.

Each SNOMED International Member country is entitled to two Member Forum representatives.

### Membership <a href="#h.fq49jrz8vl2y_l" id="h.fq49jrz8vl2y_l"></a>

Each SNOMED International Member country may nominate up to two representatives to participate in the Member Forum.

Representatives serve as the link between their national SNOMED CT community and the international Member Forum.&#x20;

Members are expected to actively participate in discussions and contribute perspectives from their national implementation environments.

### Who are Member Forum Representatives?

Member Forum representatives are appointed by their member organisation and are typically those leading or closely involved in SNOMED CT implementation at a national level. A MF representative is typically someone with:

* **Decision-making authority** - the ability to make or influence decisions related to SNOMED CT implementation within their country
* **Implementation expertise** - hands-on experience leading or managing national SNOMED CT implementation programmes
* **Digital health knowledge** - a strong understanding of the digital health landscape, whether from a clinical, technical, or policy perspective
* **Standards connectivity** - connections and influence with other standards organisations and alliances, enabling broader collaboration
* **Diverse perspectives** - the Member Forum actively encourages diverse membership in all respects

**Examples of typical Member Forum representatives include:**

* National Release Centre (NRC) leads or staff
* National SNOMED CT implementation programme leads
* Digital health programme managers or advisors
* Chief Health Information Officers
* Representatives from standards organisations and digital health alliances
* Ministry of Health digital health leads

### Leadership <a href="#h.fpvccpi1orbg_l" id="h.fpvccpi1orbg_l"></a>

The Member Forum is chaired by two Co-Chairs:

* A SNOMED International representative
* A Member country representative

The Co-Chairs help guide discussions, coordinate activities, and represent the Member Forum in interactions with SNOMED International leadership and governance groups.

### Meetings <a href="#h.d71r7rv1cbbm_l" id="h.d71r7rv1cbbm_l"></a>

The Member Forum meets regularly in two formats.

#### Virtual Meetings

Virtual meetings are typically held every two months to:

* Discuss current topics
* Share national updates
* Review initiatives and collaborative activities

#### In-Person Meetings

The Member Forum also meets during the SNOMED International Business Meetings, typically held in:

* April
* October

These sessions often include workshops, collaboration activities, and strategic discussions.

More information: https://snomed.org/events

### Agendas and Materials <a href="#h.9qb7ivvwijih_l" id="h.9qb7ivvwijih_l"></a>

Meeting agendas are typically published at least 10 working days before meetings.

Supporting materials and meeting documentation are shared through the Member Forum Confluence space.

### Proposing Agenda Items <a href="#h.6tfu14mc247k_l" id="h.6tfu14mc247k_l"></a>

Member Forum representatives are encouraged to propose discussion topics or agenda items.

Topics can be submitted via the Member Forum Confluence Space under: “Propose an Agenda Item.”

Suggested topics may include:

* Implementation challenges
* National experiences
* Requests for collaboration
* Strategic discussion topics
* New initiatives or working groups

## Your Role as a Member Forum Representative <a href="#h.bko47evjssq1_l" id="h.bko47evjssq1_l"></a>

As a Member Forum representative, you play an important role in connecting your national SNOMED CT community with the international community.

Responsibilities typically include:

* Participating in Member Forum meetings and discussions
* Sharing national implementation experiences
* Contributing to collaborative discussions and working groups
* Providing advice and feedback to SNOMED International
* Communicating relevant outcomes to your General Assembly representative and national stakeholders
* Responding to briefing notes by collating and consolidating your country's views and opinions

***

## Communication Expectations <a href="#h.nu28p07obvc2_l" id="h.nu28p07obvc2_l"></a>

Member Forum representatives are expected to:

* Respond to requests for feedback from SNOMED International, the Co-Chairs or fellow Member Forum members
* Participate in discussions in the Member Forum discussion forum
* Stay informed about ongoing work and shared resources (such as briefing notes)
* Share relevant discussions and outcomes with stakeholders in their national community

## Working with Governance and Stakeholder Groups <a href="#h.3tghmue0wj64_l" id="h.3tghmue0wj64_l"></a>

### Working with the General Assembly (GA) <a href="#h.ctfl0fvchnyl_l" id="h.ctfl0fvchnyl_l"></a>

**Focus: Organisational governance and formal decision-making**

The General Assembly (GA) is the highest decision-making body of SNOMED International. It is composed of representatives from each Member country and is responsible for approving strategy, policies, and major organisational decisions.

The Member Forum supports the GA by providing implementation insights, shared experiences, and perspectives from national SNOMED CT programs. Discussions within the Member Forum can help identify common challenges, emerging needs, and opportunities that may inform GA discussions and decisions.

#### Collaboration with Your GA Representative

As a Member Forum representative, it is important to maintain regular communication and collaboration with the General Assembly representative from your country.

While the GA representative participates in formal governance and decision-making, the Member Forum representative brings implementation experience and operational perspectives from the national SNOMED CT community.

Working closely together helps ensure that:

* Insights from Member Forum discussions are shared with your country’s GA representative when relevant
* Your national implementation experiences inform broader governance discussions
* Outcomes and decisions from the General Assembly are communicated back to the Member Forum and the wider national community

Strong collaboration between Member Forum representatives and GA representatives helps ensure that Member perspectives are well represented in both operational collaboration and formal governance discussions across SNOMED International.

### Working with the Senior Management Team (SMT) <a href="#h.nfm21n4n06jq_l" id="h.nfm21n4n06jq_l"></a>

**Focus: Operational leadership and strategy delivery**

The Senior Management Team (SMT) leads the day-to-day operations of SNOMED International. They are responsible for implementing the organisation’s strategy, managing resources, and overseeing organisational performance.

#### How the Member Forum Engages

Member Forum discussions often provide valuable implementation feedback and operational insights that can inform SMT activities.

Member Forum representatives may:

* Share member feedback on operational impact
* Raise practical implementation concerns
* Seek clarification on initiatives or services
* Provide insights from national SNOMED CT programs

Engagement Opportunities

* Participate in discussions with SMT representatives during Member Forum meetings
* Attend SMT presentations during SNOMED International Business Meetings
* Share feedback or questions raised by Member communities
* Provide practical perspectives to help inform operational decisions

### Working with the Management Board (MB) <a href="#h.wb6mepg7upnj_l" id="h.wb6mepg7upnj_l"></a>

**Focus: Strategic oversight and governance**

The Management Board (MB) is the elected governance body responsible for overseeing SNOMED International between General Assembly sessions.

The MB acts on delegated authority from the GA and ensures that decisions and policies approved by the GA are implemented. It provides strategic oversight and helps bridge governance discussions between the GA and the SMT.

#### How the Member Forum Contributes

The Member Forum supports governance discussions by providing member perspectives on strategic topics and implementation considerations.

Member Forum representatives may contribute by:

* Providing member perspectives on strategic issues
* Identifying potential impacts of proposed initiatives
* Sharing implementation experiences that may inform strategic decisions
* Highlighting emerging needs or challenges across Member countries

Engagement Opportunities

* Review Management Board reports and updates shared during meetings
* Participate in discussions that relate to strategic priorities
* Share insights from national experiences that may inform governance decisions
* Maintain dialogue with governance representatives where appropriate
