# About Clinical Reference Groups

## **Clinical Reference Groups**

As a clinical terminology, SNOMED CT must contain content that is relevant, accurate, and up to date. Clinical Reference Groups (CRGs) are essential to achieving this, bringing together clinical expertise from around the world to ensure that the content clinicians need is included in SNOMED CT quickly and accurately.

Clinical Reference Groups provide a framework for discussion between clinicians focused on specific clinical specialties or topic areas, and between clinicians and SNOMED International. Each CRG has a dedicated SNOMED Forum supporting discussions and providing information relevant to the specialty area.

## **What CRGs provide**

* A framework for multi-disciplinary discussion and collaboration across professions
* Access to expertise from a wide range of clinical domains
* Different levels of engagement to suit your availability and interest
* A connection to discussions on clinical developments by implementers and vendors, ensuring products can be effectively used within systems
* Early identification of global changes in healthcare delivery

## **Who can get involved?**

CRGs are primarily aimed at clinicians with an interest in the specialty area or topic, but are open to everyone. We actively encourage input from subject matter experts, including clinicians, implementers, vendors, and information specialists. Engaging through CRGs gives you a direct mechanism to influence SNOMED CT content and share experience with colleagues globally, avoiding duplication of effort as SNOMED CT becomes increasingly embedded in day-to-day clinical practice.

## **How to get involved**

CRG pages on SNOMED Spaces are publicly available and can be viewed without an account.

To participate in group discussions, which are hosted on [**SNOMED Forums**](https://forums.snomed.org/c/clinical-groups/20), you will need to create a free account; simply select **"Create Account"** and follow the self-registration process.

Browse the [**Clinical Reference Group Directory**](https://conf.spaces.snomed.org/wiki/spaces/CP/pages/131532679) to find a group that matches your area of interest and start engaging today.
