# About Advisory Groups

SNOMED International has an active ecosystem of advisory groups that conduct specific activities contributing to the fulfilment of the organization’s responsibilities, operational work items as well as the organization’s mandate. Advisory groups are chaired by SNOMED International staff and are agile in nature, given the changing needs of our organization.

Playing a critical role in the management and direction of organization priorities, each advisory group initiates the year by developing a group work plan outline, determining the process by which work plans are actioned, outlining applicable resource requirements, and laying out a set of criteria against which to measure the progress of the applicable group work plan.

## Structure and Purpose

Advisory Groups are established to support the Management Team in fulfilling its responsibilities and advancing the organisation's mandate. Each group is agile in nature and reviews occur to see whether changes to its Terms of Reference are required.

Each Advisory Group is expected to:

* Develop a work plan
* Determine whether Task and Finish sub-groups are required
* Review and update their Terms of Reference&#x20;

### Principles

SNOMED International is founded on the principles of **openness, transparency, communication, and accountability**. Advisory Group meetings are open to experts from around the world, and Terms of Reference are posted publicly. Members are responsible for identifying, documenting, and managing any conflicts of interest appropriately.

### Terms of Service

* For some advisory groups Members serve a **two-year term**, renewable once, with a maximum of **five cumulative years** across all Advisory Groups unless approved by the CEO and Management Board
* Terms run from **1 October to 30 September**
* A staggered appointment process ensures approximately 50% turnover in any given cycle, maintaining continuity

### Ways of Working

* **Face-to-face meetings** are held approximately twice a year
* **Conference calls** are scheduled at the Chair's discretion, with timing based on members' time zones
* Meeting agendas are shared at least **three days in advance** of calls and **one week in advance** of face-to-face meetings
* Meeting notes are posted on **SNOMED Spaces** as a public record
* Members must declare conflicts of interest, recorded in meeting notes and on the Declaration of Interests page
* Advisory Group meetings are open to **observers**, who may contribute but may not vote
* **SNOMED Forums** is the primary platform for Advisory Group discussions on key topics; members are encouraged to participate regularly.

## Recruitment Process

Advisory Group recruitment is administered by a **Nominating Committee**, consisting of AG Chairs with final approval by the CEO. Recruitment takes place between the April and October business meetings.

There are two nomination processes and it is important to understand which applies to each Advisory Group:

### **Expert Nomination Process**

The expert nomination process is an **open process** available to anyone from the Community of Practice, Member countries, vendors, or other stakeholders.

* Positions are advertised openly and applications are accepted and collated by the Corporate Services Team
* Selection is based on a **skills matrix** developed by the AG Chair, which forms the basis for assessing candidates
* The Nominating Committee selects candidates based on skills, experience, and a desire for diversity of perspectives and regional representation
* **Travel expenses** to attend approved meetings are reimbursed by SNOMED International

The following Advisory Groups use the expert nomination process:

* SNOMED CT Editorial AG
* Modeling AG
* Terminology Release AG

### **Member-Based Nomination Process**

The member-based nomination process is used when SNOMED International seeks to work closely with its Members. Rather than an open call, **Member Forum representatives are contacted directly** and asked to nominate a candidate on behalf of their country.

* The nominated candidate should have the skills and experience outlined in the position profile developed by the AG Chair
* SNOMED International accepts Member nominees subject only to term length and participation requirements
* **Travel expenses** are the responsibility of the sponsoring Member country

The following Advisory Groups use the member-based nomination process:

* Content Managers AG
* E-Learning AG

### **What Both Processes Have in Common**

Regardless of the nomination process, all Advisory Group members are expected to have:

* A thorough understanding of the Advisory Group's responsibilities
* High ethical standards and integrity
* The ability to work collaboratively as a team member
* A willingness to abide by the Conflict of Interest Policy
