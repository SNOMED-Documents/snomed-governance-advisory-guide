# Advisory Groups

## Overview

SNOMED International's Advisory Groups are at the heart of how we develop and improve SNOMED CT. They bring together experts, Member country representatives, and community members to shape the future of the terminology. Whether you have expertise in clinical content, modeling, release processes, or education, there is likely a group where your skills and experience can make a real difference.

***

## **SNOMED CT Editorial Advisory Group**

**What they do**\
The Editorial AG provides expert advice and guidance on the editorial policies that underpin the development and maintenance of SNOMED CT content. The group investigates and resolves complex editorial issues, reviews the SNOMED CT Editorial Guide, and provides independent second opinions on key content decisions.

**Who should join**\
This group is ideal for experienced terminologists or those with significant expertise in SNOMED CT content development and maintenance.

**How to join**\
Membership is through the **expert nomination process**. Positions are advertised openly and selection is based on a skills matrix. Contact Corporate Services for more information on upcoming vacancies.

***

## **Modeling Advisory Group**

**What they do**\
The Modeling AG ensures SNOMED International has access to the latest thinking on clinical knowledge representation and information modeling. The group provides balanced, practical advice on how clinical knowledge is structured and used, covering areas such as description logic, data modeling, and the practical application of SNOMED CT across the health industry.

**Who should join**\
This group welcomes experts from clinical domains, logic and science, information modeling, and the software vendor community.

**How to join**\
Membership is through the **expert nomination process** and is skills-based. If you have expertise in any of the domains above, look out for advertised vacancies or contact Corporate Services to express your interest.

***

## **Terminology Release Advisory Group**

**What they do**\
The Terminology Release AG advises on the standards, processes, and documentation related to the production, quality assurance, packaging, and distribution of SNOMED CT and its derivative products. The group also advises on the implications of release changes for Members and software vendors, and on the introduction of new terminology products.

**Who should join**\
This group is well suited to those with experience in terminology release management, software development, or standards related to SNOMED CT distribution.

**How to join**\
Membership is through the **expert nomination process**. Contact Corporate Services for information on how to apply when positions become available.

***

## **Content Managers Advisory Group**

**What they do**\
The Content Managers AG (CMAG) is a collaborative forum where representatives from Member countries work together and with SNOMED International on content development priorities and national extension management. The group provides input on content projects, responds to briefing notes, and supports national content managers in sharing knowledge and best practice.

**Who should join**\
This group is open to representatives from all Member countries, particularly those involved in national SNOMED CT content management or extension development. It is a great entry point for getting involved in shaping SNOMED CT content at both a national and international level.

**How to join**\
Each Member country can nominate a representative through their **Member Forum representative**. Speak to your MF representative to find out more about nominating someone from your country.

***

## **E-Learning Advisory Group**

**What they do**\
The E-Learning AG shapes the development and delivery of SNOMED International's e-learning products and services. The group advises on educational priorities, supports the sharing and translation of learning resources between Member countries, and helps ensure that training meets the needs of Members and the wider SNOMED CT community.

**Who should join**\
This group is ideal for those with an interest in education, training, and knowledge development related to SNOMED CT. It is a great opportunity to influence how SNOMED CT education is delivered globally and to support learning initiatives in your own country.

**How to join**\
Most positions are filled through the **Member-based nomination process**, with Member countries nominating representatives. Contact your MF representative to find out how to get involved.

***

For general information about joining any of the Advisory Groups, or to find out about upcoming vacancies, please contact info@snomed.org.
