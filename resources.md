# Resources

The following resources have been selected to help you access key documents, deepen your knowledge of SNOMED CT, and provide content to share with others in your country; all to support you in your role as a governance or advisory group member

## Documents

The SNOMED CT Document Library is a comprehensive resource covering general SNOMED CT information, technical documentation, and implementation guidance. To get you started, the following documents are particularly useful:

* [**SNOMED CT Starter Guide**](https://docs.snomed.org/snomed-ct-practical-guides/snomed-ct-starter-guide)
* [**SNOMED NRC Guide**](https://docs.snomed.org/snomed-ct-practical-guides/snomed-nrc-guide)
* [**SNOMED CT EHR Requirements Guide**](https://docs.snomed.org/snomed-ct-practical-guides/snomed-ct-ehr-requirements-guide)

The full document library can be accessed here:

<a href="https://docs.snomed.org/" class="button primary" data-icon="book-open-lines">SNOMED CT Document Library</a>

## SNOMED CT Engagement Resources

We have compiled a set of presentations to support you in promoting SNOMED CT within your country. The presentations cover the following topics:

* What is SNOMED CT?
* Why is it needed?
* The Scope of SNOMED CT
* Benefits of SNOMED CT (Part 1 and 2)
* Secondary Use Benefits
* About SNOMED International

These presentations are available in the **GA and MF Engagement Resources** folder, which is accessible to GA and MF representatives only.

<a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WDVmS72kCtaU602k3QOXRvh9FuTATZPW" class="button primary" data-icon="folder">GA and MF Engagement Resources</a>

#### SNOMED CT Use Cases - YouTube Search Tool

To explore real-world applications of SNOMED CT, we have developed a YouTube search tool that searches across videos created by SNOMED International and the SNOMED Community of Practice. Use it to discover:

* SNOMED CT activity in your country
* Specific use cases and topics of interest, such as ECL or AI
* Videos from particular webinar series
