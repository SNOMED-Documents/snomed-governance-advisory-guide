#### Key Functional Areas

SNOMED International’s work is delivered through several specialised functional areas that support the development, implementation, and global adoption of SNOMED CT.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/SNOMED International Teams (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Together, these teams ensure that SNOMED International can effectively maintain SNOMED CT and support its global implementation.

<details>

<summary>SNOMED International Teams</summary>

**Content:** Responsible for maintaining and expanding the SNOMED CT terminology, including clinical content development and quality assurance.

**Mapping:** Supports the development of maps between SNOMED CT and other clinical terminologies and classifications to facilitate interoperability and data reuse.

**Technical:** Develops and maintains tools, infrastructure, and services that support SNOMED CT distribution, terminology management, and implementation.

**Collaboration:** Facilitates engagement with Members and partner organizations to support collaboration across the global SNOMED community.

**Education:** Provides training materials, courses, and learning resources to support understanding and implementation of SNOMED CT.

**Communication and Events:** Manages organizational communications, community engagement, and the coordination of events such as SNOMED International Business Meetings.

**Clinical Engagement**: Engages with clinicians and subject matter experts to ensure SNOMED CT reflects real-world clinical practice and supports clinical use.

**Vendor Engagement:** Works with health IT vendors and solution providers to support the integration and adoption of SNOMED CT in healthcare systems.

**Customer Relations:** Supports Members and stakeholders by responding to inquiries, coordinating requests, and helping ensure successful use of SNOMED CT resources.

**Implementation Support:** Provides guidance, documentation, and support to Members and implementers to help facilitate successful SNOMED CT adoption.

Core organizational services, including management, finance, human resources, and corporate services, support the day-to-day operation of the organization and underpin the work of all the teams.



</details>
