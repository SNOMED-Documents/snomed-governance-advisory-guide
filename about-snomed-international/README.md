# About SNOMED International

SNOMED International is a not-for-profit organisation that owns and maintains SNOMED CT, the world’s most comprehensive clinical terminology. Its mission is to enable global health information interoperability by supporting the consistent representation and exchange of clinical data.

## <mark style="color:$primary;">Member Pages</mark>

SNOMED International has grown to over 50 members and has issued Affiliate Licenses to more than 50,000 individuals and organizations.&#x20;

Each Member has a page on the SNOMED International website that provides key information about the National Release Center and the General Assembly and Member Forum representatives. &#x20;
