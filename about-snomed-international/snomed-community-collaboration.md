## <mark style="color:$primary;">Collaboration Platforms</mark>

We have several platforms to support collaboration and communication across the SNOMED International community.

**Your SNOMED International Account**\
Your account serves as your central landing page, giving you access to all key SNOMED International resources, including SNOMED Spaces, Forums, and tools relevant to your role and requirements.

<a href="https://snoauth.ihtsdotools.org/" class="button primary" data-icon="user">SNOMED International Account</a>

**SNOMED Spaces**\
SNOMED Spaces is an essential platform for anyone in an advisory or governance role. This is where you will find key documents, meeting agendas, and other important resources for your group.

<a href="https://conf.spaces.snomed.org/" class="button primary" data-icon="confluence">SNOMED Spaces</a>

**SNOMED Forums**\
SNOMED Forums is the primary communication platform for governance and advisory groups and serves as a discussion space for the wider SNOMED CT Community of Practice.

<a href="https://forums.snomed.org/" class="button primary" data-icon="discourse">SNOMED Forums</a>
