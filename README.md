# SNOMED International Governance and Advisory Handbook

This guide is the starting point for individuals joining governance bodies, advisory groups, and other collaborative roles within SNOMED International.&#x20;

Beyond general orientation, this guide also includes dedicated sections tailored to specific governance bodies and stakeholder groups, offering in-depth guidance on roles, responsibilities, and priorities relevant to your particular position.

These resources provide key information and guidance to help new stakeholders quickly understand SNOMED’s mission, governance structure, and ways of working.&#x20;

It brings together essential materials, orientation content, and practical tools to support members in contributing effectively and confidently to the SNOMED community from the outset.
