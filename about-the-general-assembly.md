# About the General Assembly

## <mark style="color:$primary;">Welcome to the General Assembly</mark> <a href="#h.w0lt2gcagh1x_l" id="h.w0lt2gcagh1x_l"></a>

SNOMED International is a 'Members-First' organization and our Members play a critical governance role in the direction of the organization. Each Member is entitled to be represented by an individual on the General Assembly.&#x20;

## What is the General Assembly?

The General Assembly is SNOMED International's highest authority. The General Assembly ensures that the purpose, objects and principles of the Association are pursued and that the interests of the organization are safeguarded. The General Assembly makes binding decisions regarding all matters, including the organization's budget, business plan and strategy, subject to and in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Association.

The General Assembly represents the interests of the Members of SNOMED International.

***

## How does the General Assembly work?

### Governing Body <a href="#h.ymwkf8nn1s3u_l" id="h.ymwkf8nn1s3u_l"></a>

The General Assembly (GA) is the supreme decision-making body of the organisation. It represents all members and sets the strategic direction.

### Purpose

The GA approves the budget, business plan and key organizational policies, elects leadership, debates motions, and holds the organization accountable to its values and mandate.

### Who is Involved

Each SNOMED International Member country may choose one General Assembly Member.

Nominated Representatives are expected to have the power to ensure the adoption of SNOMED in the Member Country.&#x20;

The elected and appointed representatives from member countries are the voice of that country and have a vote on the decisions made at the General Assembly.

The Executive Committee.

The GA elect members to the following executive committee positions:

* GA Chair
* GA Vice Chair
* Chair Emeritus/Emerita (Past chair)&#x20;

If possible the three regions (AsiaPac, EMEA and the Americas) should be represented on the Executive Committee.

### Meetings

**Types of Meetings**

**Ordinary General Assembly Meetings**

These are held every April and October. These meetings are where key decisions are made and all GA members can attend.

**Informal GA Meetings and GA/MB Meetings**

Can be called for general session meetings and to plan meeting schedules for the biannual business meetings.

**Extraordinary GA Meetings and electronic voting**

Called for urgent matters requiring full GA input or votes.

**Committee Meetings**

Smaller working groups preparing GA business between sessions.

**Meeting Packs**

Meeting packs required for proceedings are sent to GA members by SNOMED International prior to each meeting.

### Voting

Normal GA votes are decided by a simple majority.&#x20;

Some matters may require a Super majority this means there needs to be two thirds of all represented members.

**Quorum**

More than 50% of GA members need to be present (In person, By Proxy, By teleconference).

**Proxies and Observers**

* Proxies have to be nominated prior to meetings by the GA member not by the proxy themselves
* GA member provides information about any Observers and only 1 observer is allowed

## Who are General Assembly Members?

General Assembly members are nominated representatives selected by their member organization based on their qualifications, experience, and ability to represent their country's interests. A GA representative is typically someone with:

* **Leadership experience -** the seniority and influence to drive decisions and advocate for SNOMED CT adoption at a national level
* **Health informatics knowledge** - a strong understanding of health informatics, whether from a clinical, technical, or policy background
* **National health system expertise** - a deep understanding of how their country's health system operates, its priorities, and its challenges
* **Decision-making authority** - the standing to influence or ensure the adoption of SNOMED CT within their country

**Examples of typical GA representatives include:**

* Chief Medical Information Officers (CMIOs)
* National Health Informatics leads
* Ministry of Health representatives or advisors
* Clinical informaticists in senior roles
* National eHealth or digital health programme leads

## Your Role as a General Assembly Member

As a General Assembly Member you are a member of the highest authority in SNOMED International. You have a very important role to play in the organization and the global SNOMED community.&#x20;

<mark style="color:cyan;">**Key responsibilities include:**</mark>

* Representing the interests of SNOMED International Members overseeing strategy, performance and finances
* Being empowered to make binding decisions regarding all matters of the organization
* Reviewing and Approving financial and strategic decisions
* Ecouraging an Active Community of Practice
* Representing SNOMED International's interests in your member country
* Coordinating interoperability and an increase in implementation of SNOMED CT at national and international level

<mark style="color:cyan;">**Engaging with others:**</mark>

* Attend biannual meetings and participate actively (either in person or by teleconference)
* Coordinate with other GA members
* Coordinate with Member Forum Representative and other NRC representatives

## Working with Governance and Stakeholder Groups

### Senior Management Team - SMT

**Focus: Operational leadership and strategy delivery**

The Senior Management Team leads day-to-day operations of SNOMED International. They are responsible for implementing the organization's strategy, resource management, and the performance of the organizational.

The Management Board acts as the bridge between the General Assembly's strategic authority and the Management Team's operational execution.

* In cooperation with MB Executive Committee and SMT develop and decide GA, GA/MB agendas

### Management Board - MB

**Focus: Strategic oversight and governance**

The Management Board (MB) is the elected governance body responsible for overseeing SNOMED International between General Assembly sessions.

The MB acts on delegated authority from the GA and ensures that decisions and policies approved by the GA are implemented. It provides strategic oversight and helps bridge governance discussions between the GA and the SMT.

How the General Assembly works with the Management Board:

* The GA ensures that the MB is performing effectively
* Review Management Board reports
* Review any proposals and consider how they will affect members
* Support accountability through discussion
* Participate in elections and nominations for MB positions
* Represent your Member country's own national strategic perspective.

GA Executive Committee and the Management Board

* Attend MB meetings
* Coordinate with MB Executive Committee on governance matters
* In cooperation with MB Executive Committee and SMT develop and decide GA, GA/MB agendas

### Member Forum - MF

**Focus: Operational Engagement and Advisory**

The Member Forum (MF) is a collaborative group made up of representatives from SNOMED International Member countries.

Its primary purpose is to enable Members to share experiences, collaborate on implementation, and provide advice to SNOMED International on matters relating to the adoption and use of SNOMED CT.

The Member Forum supports the GA by providing implementation insights, shared experiences, and perspectives from national SNOMED CT programs. Discussions within the Member Forum can help identify common challenges, emerging needs, and opportunities that may inform GA discussions and decisions.

Working with Member Forum Representatives:

* As a General Assembly Member you are the conduit between the MF and the formal GA decision making process.
* Consult as often as possible with your MF representative especially before in person meetings
* Discuss Member priorities with the MF representative to understand your national SNOMED CT priorities
* Communicate outcomes and decisions with Member Forum representatives and your wider national SNOMED CT community

Strong collaboration between Member Forum representatives and GA representatives helps ensure that Member perspectives are well represented in both operational collaboration and formal governance discussions across SNOMED International.
